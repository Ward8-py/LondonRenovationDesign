Design, Supply & Installation
We offer a comprehensive kitchen and bathroom service which means we take care of planning, designing and installation. we can even design and build a customised kitchen to suite your requirements.
If you purchase from us or elsewhere, we offer a complete installation service using our highly trained and qualified team of fitters who have many years of experience in taking great care to turn your dream kitchen into reality.
We have designed 3 simple steps to bring your dream kitchen & or bathroom into reality.
1. Consultation & planning
Every new kitchen or bathroom will begin with a site visit from one of our surveyors. here is where we get the chance to meet you and listen to your ideas that will give us an understanding of the requirements and needs. we will then establish the functionality you require from your kitchen or bathroom. we will then aim to create innovative designs which will help you to achieve your inspirations that we will then turn into reality.
2. Designing your Kitchen & or Bathroom
After we have gained a full understanding of your requirements and needs, we then begin designing your new kitchen or bathroom. The design enables you to view how the kitchen or bathroom will actually look in reality, 3D images will be created from different angles to give you a touch and feel of your new kitchen or bathroom.
Viewing the plans and designs will then enables you to fully visualise how your new kitchen or bathroom will look like which benefits you and gives you the flexibility if you wish to amend the design by layout or styling.
We are a supplier for a major kitchen company who have excellent reputation for producing high quality and well designed kitchens. One of our main reasons why we can offer a highly competitive prices on kitchen products is because these kitchens are only available to the building trade to fully qualified Kitchen specialists contractors.
3. Kitchen or Bathroom estimation
After you have viewed your kitchen & or bathroom designs and confirmed approval. We will then generate a quotation for supply & install. Fire damage restoration
2hr Emergency call out anywhere within London and surrounding areas.

Where do we start?

Emergency Contact – The process starts when you call us using our contact details. Calls are answered 24/7.
Step 2:
Inspection and Fire Damage Assessment – Trained team will inspect your property to determine the extent of the fire and damages caused as a result of the fire.
Step 3:
Board-up and Roof cover Service (if needed) – Fire Damage can often damage windows, walls and roofs. In order to prevent further damage to the property we can at your request board up all damaged windows and cover the roof top so that no further damage is caused by weather conditions.
Step 4:
Water Removal and Drying (if water damage is present) – The water removal starts immediately by pumping all water away. Once this is done we will install drying equipment and dehumidifiers to remove the remaining water and complete the drying process.
Step 5:
Removal of Smoke and Soot from All Surfaces – We will install special equipment and remove smoke and soot from walls and ceiling within your property internally and externally.
Step 6:
Cleaning – The cleaning process starts by removing everything from the site and or the damaged areas to enable us and the client to fully view the damage caused and discuss all the relevant steps leading to the restoration stages.
Step 7:
Restoration – This stage involves conducting a full restoration report to detail what is damaged and what needs to be repaired or restored. This stage involves several meetings with the client to address all the needs and discuss how we will work to meet the client needs and wants.
